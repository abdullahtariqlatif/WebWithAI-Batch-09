# Home Buying Guide (sample knowledge base)

> Illustrative content for learning RAG. Verify real rules, taxes and fees with a professional before any transaction.

## Steps to buy a property
1. **Set a budget.** Include the price plus roughly 5–8% extra for transfer fees, taxes, agent commission and legal costs.
2. **Shortlist areas.** Compare commute, schools, security, water/gas supply and resale demand.
3. **Visit in person** at different times of day. Check for seepage, cracks, wiring and water pressure.
4. **Verify documents.** Confirm the seller's ownership, check the title documents, the society NOC, and make sure there are no pending dues or legal disputes.
5. **Pay a token / bayana** only against a signed agreement that states the full price, payment schedule and penalties.
6. **Transfer** the property at the relevant authority (society office or land registry) and get the documents in your own name.

## Common mistakes
- Paying cash without written receipts.
- Buying in a society that is not approved by the development authority.
- Ignoring the cost of renovation for older homes.

## Plot vs. built house
A plot lets you design your own home and often appreciates faster, but construction takes 8–14 months and costs rise with material prices. A built house lets you move in immediately and is easier to rent out.
