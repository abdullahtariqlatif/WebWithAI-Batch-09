# Home Financing Guide (sample knowledge base)

> Illustrative content for learning RAG. Rates change often — always check current bank offers.

## How a home loan (mortgage) works
You pay a **down payment** (usually 15–30% of the price) and the bank finances the rest. You repay the loan in monthly instalments over a **tenure** (commonly 5–25 years). Each instalment covers interest plus part of the principal.

Monthly payment formula: `M = P * r * (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n - 1)` where P is the loan amount, r the monthly interest rate and n the number of months.

## Islamic home financing
Many banks offer Shariah-compliant options such as **Diminishing Musharakah**, where the bank and buyer co-own the house and the buyer gradually buys the bank's share while paying rent on the remaining portion.

## Affordability rule of thumb
Keep the monthly instalment below about 35–40% of take-home household income. Banks will also look at your credit history, job stability and existing loans.

## Documents usually required
National ID, salary slips or business income proof, bank statements (6–12 months), and the property's title documents.
