# Neighborhood Notes (sample knowledge base)

> Illustrative content for learning RAG.

## Lahore
- **DHA (Phases 1–9):** Planned, secure, high demand; premium prices. Phases 5 and 6 are mature with good schools and commercial areas. Phase 8 is close to the airport.
- **Gulberg:** Central business and dining hub. Mostly apartments and commercial property; great for young professionals.
- **Johar Town:** Mid-range, close to Emporium Mall, Expo Centre and the Orange Line metro. Good value for first-time buyers.
- **Bahria Town Lahore:** Gated community with its own amenities; popular with families wanting security at mid-range prices.
- **Lake City:** Newer society on Raiwind Road; plots are popular as investments.

## Karachi
- **Clifton & DHA:** Upscale, sea-facing, high-rise apartments and bungalows.
- **Gulshan-e-Iqbal:** Dense, central, affordable flats; close to universities.
- **PECHS:** Older, central residential area with large plots.
- **Bahria Town Karachi:** Large gated development on the Super Highway; far from the city centre but well-planned.

## Islamabad & Rawalpindi
- **F and E sectors (F-6, F-7, E-7, E-11):** Prime, green, close to the Margalla Hills; the most expensive areas.
- **G sectors (G-11, G-13):** Family-friendly, mid-range, well-served by markets.
- **H-13:** Near universities like NUST; lots of student rentals.
- **Bahria Town / Bahria Enclave:** Gated, amenity-rich communities between Islamabad and Rawalpindi.
- **Saddar (Rawalpindi):** Busy commercial centre with Metro Bus access.
