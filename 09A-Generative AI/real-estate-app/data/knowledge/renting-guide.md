# Renting Guide (sample knowledge base)

> Illustrative content for learning RAG.

## Typical rental terms
- **Security deposit:** commonly 2–3 months of rent, refundable at the end of the lease minus damages.
- **Advance rent:** many landlords ask for 1–2 months of rent in advance.
- **Lease length:** usually 11 months or 1 year, renewable with a 5–10% annual increase.
- **Agent fee:** often equal to half a month's to one month's rent.

## Checklist before signing
- Read the rent agreement and keep a signed copy.
- Register the tenancy with the local police station where required (tenant registration).
- Photograph the property's condition on move-in day.
- Clarify who pays utility bills, maintenance charges and minor repairs.

## Upper portion vs. full house
An upper portion is cheaper and often has a separate entrance, but you share the building and sometimes the water tank with the landlord's family. A full house gives privacy and a lawn but costs more.
